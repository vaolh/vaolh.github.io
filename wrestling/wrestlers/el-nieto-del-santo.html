El Nieto del Santo (born Mar 8, 1996) is a Mexican professional wrestler. He performs under a masked técnico ("good guy") character and is widely regarded as one of the fastest-rising and most accomplished prospects in the history of professional wrestling.

At LibreMania, El Nieto del Santo retained the World Wrestling Federation (WWF) world middleweight championship while winning the World Wrestling Organization (WWO) and The Ring world middleweight championships. In doing so, he became the first unified champion of the World Title Series era and the first wrestler to simultaneously hold the WWF, WWO, and The Ring middleweight titles.

El Nieto del Santo is the third-generation wrestler to use the "Santo" ring name, following El Santo and El Hijo del Santo. His real name is not a matter of public record, as is customary for masked wrestlers in Mexico, where performers traditionally keep their identities private. Although his relationship to the Santo family has never been officially confirmed, El Hijo del Santo has publicly endorsed him on multiple occasions, and wrestling promotions have repeatedly hinted that he is a member of the Guzmán wrestling family while maintaining the secrecy surrounding his identity.
