The Draft

The Draft
Sport Professional wrestling
Presented by The Ring
Format Snake draft
Rounds 60 (180 picks)
Divisions Men's and women's
Bodies WWF, WWO, IWB
First draft 2020

The Draft is an annual professional wrestling event, presented by The Ring, in which the three sanctioning bodies of the unified ruleset—the World Wrestling Federation (WWF), the World Wrestling Organization (WWO), and the International Wrestling Board (IWB)—each select ten challengers per weight division from a shared pool of eligible wrestlers. Selections lock a wrestler to that body for the season and form the field of mandatory challengers who pursue its champions across the year's World Title Series. Held on the second Saturday of January, the first Draft under the current format took place in 2020, following the unification agreement that consolidated the sport's world championships under a single calendar.

The Draft is conducted as a snake draft across the entire eligible pool: each round every body makes one selection, and the pick order reverses every round so that no organization is permanently advantaged. Reigning champions are not entered into the Draft; instead they are pre-locked to their body and defend against the ten challengers drafted to their division, the field that also feeds the year's number-one contender battle royals and singles eliminators.

Selection priority is weighted toward spreading opportunity across the rankings and toward wrestlers who have been inactive, rather than concentrating the strongest names, so that every division is refreshed from season to season. Wrestlers left undrafted remain free agents, eligible to be signed for special-attraction bouts or returned to contention in a later Draft, while those who retire or lose their careers in a Lucha de Apuestas are permanently removed from the pool.
